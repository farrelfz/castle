Guidelines

CASTLE 2026 author guidelines

Use this page as a concise booklet for preparing abstracts, full papers, and supporting documents.

Start Registration

Participant Terms

General requirements

  • Open to students, researchers, academics, teachers, and practitioners.
  • A maximum of 5 members per team for collaborative manuscripts.
  • Each author may be involved in a maximum of 3 articles.
  • Manuscripts must be original and unpublished.

Academic Ethics

Publication standards

  • Avoid plagiarism, duplicate publication, and data manipulation.
  • Include citations and references according to the required format.
  • Manuscript revisions must follow reviewer notes.
  • Final publication decisions belong to the editorial team.
Format

Abstract and paper format

Follow this structure so the committee and reviewers can process manuscripts efficiently.

Abstract

250-300 words

Includes background, objective, method, key findings, and keywords.

Full Paper

4000-6000 words

Contains introduction, method, results, discussion, conclusion, and references.

Citation Style

APA Style

Use consistent citation formatting and ensure all references are traceable.

Topics

Call for papers scope

Choose the track that best matches your research contribution.

Social and Humanities

Society, policy, culture, communication, and empowerment.

Science and Technology

Science research, digital technology, innovation, and computational application.

Language and Education

Literacy, language learning, pedagogy, curriculum, and assessment.

Workflow

Submission workflow

Follow these stages from preparation to publication decision.

  1. Choose a track and prepare the abstract according to the format.
  2. Submit the abstract through the official committee channel.
  3. Wait for screening results and reviewer notes.
  4. Upload the full paper and revise if requested.
  5. Complete registration, payment, and presentation confirmation.
