Steps

Participant registration workflow

Designed so participants and presenters know each required stage.

  1. Read the booklet and select the most relevant conference track.
  2. Complete the registration form and participant or team identity.
  3. Submit the abstract, then wait for screening results and reviewer guidance.
  4. Upload the full paper if the abstract is accepted.
  5. Complete payment, upload proof, and wait for committee confirmation.
