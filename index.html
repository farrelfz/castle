About CASTLE

A formal green academic conference with publication pathways

CASTLE 2026 is hosted by Universitas Negeri Jakarta as a scientific forum that strengthens collaboration, innovation, and research-based solutions.

The sixth edition brings social, science, technology, language, and education issues into one focused academic conversation.

Participants can present research, build academic networks, and choose publication pathways aligned with manuscript quality.
